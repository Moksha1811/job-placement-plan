## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR SUCCESS

### Learning:
✅ **Start with Free**, add Kodekloud if possible
✅ **Watch YouTube videos at 1.5x speed** (save time)
✅ **Build projects while learning** (don't just watch)
✅ **Blog about everything** (forces understanding + builds credibility)

### Projects:
✅ **Make repos public** (portfolio visibility)
✅ **Add detailed README** (impressive to recruiters)
✅ **Deploy to production** (show it's real)
✅ **Get feedback** (improve before interviews)

### Job Search:
✅ **Apply to startups first** (faster hiring, better mentorship)
✅ **Reach out to recruiters** (personal touch matters)
✅ **Follow up 1 week after applying** (friendly email)
✅ **Negotiate thoughtfully** (first job is about learning)

### Interview Prep:
✅ **Mock interviews with peers** (practice makes perfect)
✅ **Record yourself answering Q's** (self-critique works)
✅ **Know your projects cold** (5-min, 15-min, 30-min versions)
✅ **Ask good questions back** (shows genuine interest)
